Controller tests use SDL virtual joysticks, including profile-specific vendor
and product identities. They exercise SDL discovery, polling, host mapping,
deadzones and guest pad delivery. They do not reproduce physical HID/XInput
drivers or radio transport. Observations assert `override=-1`; gameplay via
the virtual pad does not rely on the debug server's guest-input override.
Controller-remap persistence uses the same production binding writer/reload
API as the UI, while keyboard capture was driven through the actual UI.

During QA, the staging runner initially copied bundled `mods/state.toml` over
the private profile on restart, resetting its resolution selection. It now
copies only immutable bundled mod packages. A repeated restart confirmed the
fix. The launcher can also update controller defaults in `game.toml`; the
runner now supplies its own private config. Early failed observations are
retained in raw logs, rather than counted as successful tests.
